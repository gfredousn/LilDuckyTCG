MY POKÉMON TCG STORY

My love for Pokémon started 24 years ago. Like many fans, I first discovered Pokémon through the show. I fell in love with the characters, the adventures, and the world of Pokémon—and that love has stayed with me ever since.

In June 2026, Pokémon became something even more special to me when my family and I started collecting Pokémon cards together. What began as collecting cards quickly became a way for my wife, my daughter, and me to spend time together, have fun, and share in the excitement of every pack we open.

There’s nothing quite like the feeling of opening a pack and seeing a rare card waiting inside. Those moments, especially when we experience them together, are what make collecting so much fun for me.

My favorite era is Scarlet & Violet. I love the cards, the artwork, and everything this era has brought to the Pokémon TCG.

I created this website as a place to share my love for Pokémon with the world. It’s a reflection of something that has been part of my life for more than two decades and has now become something I get to enjoy with my family.

Whether you're a longtime collector, a casual fan, or someone just discovering Pokémon cards, I hope you enjoy exploring the site and sharing in the excitement.

This is my Pokémon story—and it's still being written.
